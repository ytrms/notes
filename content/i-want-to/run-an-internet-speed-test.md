title: Run an Internet speed test
---
Don't use websites like speedtest dot net. Instead, use one of these:
- [fast.com](https://fast.com)
- [speed.cloudflare.com](https://speed.cloudflare.com/)

# Why these?
In short, because your internet provider can't "boost" the results on those, and as a result they are more accurate.

The longer answer is that when you connect to sites like "speedtest", your internet provider can detect that and give you a boost for that connection only.

The sites I recommend on this page use the infrastructure provided by Netflix and Cloudflare. Meaning that when you run a speed test there, your provider can't tell that you're running a speedtest - they just see that you connected to Netflix/Cloudflare, so you could just be watching a movie. As a result, these are more realistic numbers.

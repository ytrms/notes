My podcast recommendations:
- [Acquired](https://overcast.fm/itunes1050462261/acquired): deep, deep dives about companies. Infrequent but long episodes.
- [Dwarkesh Podcast](https://overcast.fm/itunes1790118720/dwarkesh-podcast): host interviews interesting guests. While I don't listen to all episodes, the host comes to interviews well prepared, which I appreciate. Has episodes with many tech founders, but also historians, scientists, etc.
